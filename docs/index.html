Skill-augmented agentic RL has emerged as an effective approach to improving LLM agents on long-horizon tasks. Despite this progress, individual skills may follow distinct trajectories as the policy evolves, with some becoming redundant after the model internalizes their capabilities, others remaining valuable as external scaffolding, and still others turning harmful.
